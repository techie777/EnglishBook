# Master System Prompt: SpeakEasy 45-Day Gamified English Quest (Kids Edition: Ages 5–12)

---

## 1. Executive Pedagogical Framework & Learning Philosophy

### Mission Statement
To transform spoken English learning for Hindi-speaking and bilingual children into an engaging, multi-island digital adventure. Kids should never feel like they are doing schoolwork; instead, they are exploring an enchanted world where words are magical power gems, sentences are spells, and speaking aloud unlocks secret treasures.

### The 4-Stage Daily Micro-Curriculum Spiral
Every single day of the 45-day course adheres to an unshakeable 15-minute cognitive progression loop:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
```

## 2. 45-Day Journey Breakdown by Islands & Themes

The course is segmented into **5 Progressive Thematic Islands** (9 Days per Island). Every 9th day is an **Island Review & Mega Boss Battle**.

*   **Island 1 (Days 1–9): The Island of Self & Surroundings**
    *   *Theme:* Greetings, My Identity, Feelings, Family, Colors, and Body.
*   **Island 2 (Days 10–18): The Island of Action & Play**
    *   *Theme:* Daily actions (Eat, Play, Sleep, Run), Toys, Pets, Foods, and Polite Requests.
*   **Island 3 (Days 19–27): The Island of Home & School**
    *   *Theme:* Classroom items, Rooms, Telling time/routine, Days of the week, Weather, and Clothes.
*   **Island 4 (Days 28–36): The Island of Curiosities & Questions**
    *   *Theme:* Asking "What?", "Where?", "Who?", "Why?", Opposites (Big/Small, Hot/Cold), and Supermarket wonders.
*   **Island 5 (Days 37–45): The Island of Confident Storytellers**
    *   *Theme:* Storytelling phrases, expressing likes/dislikes, future plans (*"I will..."*), problem solving, and the Graduation Ceremony.

## 4. Kids Gamification & UX Design Blueprint for Frontend Developers

### 1. The Day-Score & Star Evaluation Engine
For each day, the child starts with **3 Hearts (❤️❤️❤️)**.
*   **Word Gem Mine:** 4 gems tapped = $+20\text{ XP}$ (5 XP each).
*   **Cauldron Formula Drill:** 3 substitutions tested = $+20\text{ XP}$.
*   **4-Way Mixed Practice:**
    *   Match the Columns: $+15\text{ XP}$ ($-1\text{ heart}$ on wrong pair).
    *   Bridge Blank: $+15\text{ XP}$.
    *   Sentence Cannon: $+15\text{ XP}$.
    *   Voice Roar (Mic): $+15\text{ XP}$ (Awarded when speech recognition confirms phonetic match).
*   **Perfect Day Completion:** $100/100$ score, $+3\text{ Golden Stars}$, $+1\text{ Daily Badge Sticker}$ added to their sticker book.

### 2. Audio-Visual Scaffolding for Non-English Native Kids
1.  **Dual Phonetic Guide:** Under every English sentence, always show:
    *   Hindi Meaning: *मेरा नाम रोहन है।*
    *   Devanagari Phonetic Pronunciation: *"माय नेम इज़ रोहन"* (Crucial for kids who cannot read English phonetics yet).
2.  **Chintu Owl Emotion System:**
    *   `happy`: Bobs gently with big smiling eyes.
    *   `thinking`: Taps beak and looks up when child pauses for $>5\text{ seconds}$.
    *   `excited`: Wings flap and graduation cap spins when a combo is hit!
3.  **Synthesized Auditory Feedback:** Never fail silently. High-chime chords for correct answers, low pleasant boing for errors (never harsh or punishing).
4.  **No Typing Mandate:** Children ages 5–12 must **never be forced to type** on a keyboard. Every single interaction is **Tap, Drag, or Speak**.
